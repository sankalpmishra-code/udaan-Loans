Questions students ask us

Do I start repaying while I study?

No. You pay only the interest or nothing during the course and a grace period, depending on the lender and plan. Full EMIs begin after that.

Can I apply before I get my admission letter?

Yes. We can check your profile and shortlist lenders early. The final sanction needs the offer letter.

Is there a tax benefit?

Interest paid on an education loan can be claimed as a deduction under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act. Please check current rules with a tax advisor.

What if my visa is rejected?

Tell your lender and us immediately. Undisbursed amounts are usually cancelled, and we help you coordinate refunds with the lender and university.

Can I repay early?

Most education loans allow part or full prepayment, and many lenders charge no penalty. Check your lender's terms.
